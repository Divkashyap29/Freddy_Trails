# Freddy Trails

> "Freddy Trails turns downtown Fredericton into a story you can only finish on foot."

A location-based story crawl through downtown Fredericton.

You start a trail, walk to a local business, and unlock a chapter of a story tied to that
spot — along with a deal from that business. To reach the next chapter, you take a photo
where you're standing and the app confirms you're really there. Walk the trail, finish the
story.

Two things make it different from a scavenger hunt with coupons:

- **The stories are grounded in real research.** Narrative arcs draw on current UNB
  Psychology and Criminology work on Atlantic Canada, so the fiction is rooted in the
  region instead of being generic AI filler.
- **No QR codes.** Visits are verified by photo, which is harder to fake from a couch and
  nicer to use than hunting for a sticker on a window.

Partner businesses get a dashboard showing the foot traffic and engagement the trail drove.
